Find Seeds

About Find Seeds

Find Seeds is part of the poweredbyci.live family of tools built for home growers. No fluff, no paywalls — just useful data.

What it does

Search 42,000+ cannabis strains across 137 seed banks and breeders. Filter by seed type, shipping region, payment method, germination guarantee, and stealth shipping — then click any vendor to see their full listing details and buy direct.

The data

The dataset is scraped and maintained manually. It covers vendor pricing, shipping regions, payment methods, germination guarantees, stealth shipping, and loyalty programs. Data is updated regularly but prices and availability change — always verify on the vendor's site before purchasing.
